Common KMPlayer Issues & Solutions

1. Video Is Not Playing

Check whether the file is complete and uses a supported format. Try another media file to identify whether the problem is related to the original file.

2. No Sound During Playback

Check your device's volume settings and confirm that the media file contains an audio track. Audio codec compatibility may also affect playback.

3. App Is Not Opening

Restart your device and check whether an app update is available. Also make sure sufficient storage space is available.

4. Video Playback Is Slow

Close unnecessary background apps and check your device's available resources. A lower-resolution media file may also play more smoothly.

5. Subtitles Are Not Appearing

Check whether subtitles are enabled and whether the subtitle file is supported. Confirm that the subtitle file matches the video.
