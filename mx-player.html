Common MX Player Issues & Solutions

1. Video Is Not Playing

Check whether the media file is complete and supported. Try another file to determine whether the issue is related to the video itself.

2. No Audio

Check your device's volume settings and make sure the video contains an audio track. Compatibility may also depend on the audio codec.

3. App Is Not Opening

Restart your device and check whether an app update is available. Make sure your device has enough free storage.

4. Playback Is Lagging

Close other running apps and try playing a lower-resolution version of the video if one is available. Performance may depend on your device's hardware.

5. Subtitle Is Not Showing

Check whether subtitles are enabled and whether the subtitle file is supported. Make sure the subtitle file corresponds to the video you are watching.
