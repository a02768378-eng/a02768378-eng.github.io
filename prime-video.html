01 / INTRODUCTION

What Is Amazon Prime Video?

Amazon Prime Video is a streaming service that offers films, television series, and other video content. Depending on the country and the title, viewers may find content included with a subscription as well as movies and shows available to rent or buy separately.

The service is available on a range of supported devices, including mobile phones, tablets, computers, selected smart TVs, and streaming devices. The experience is not identical everywhere: the catalogue, payment options, and available features can vary by region and device.

This guide focuses on the app itself, how to find the official version, and what to understand before using its streaming or download features.

Good to know: Having the app installed does not automatically mean every movie or channel is included in your membership. Check the details shown on each title before paying.

02 / FEATURES

Prime Video Features Worth Knowing

The app combines a searchable entertainment catalogue with playback and account tools. The exact options you see may depend on your device, region, membership, and the title you're watching.

FEATURE 01

Browse and discover

Search for a particular film or series, explore recommendations, and review a title's details before deciding what to watch.

FEATURE 02

Profiles

Supported versions of Prime Video offer profiles so people can maintain separate viewing preferences. Available profile controls may differ across devices.

FEATURE 03

Offline downloads

Selected titles can be downloaded in supported apps for temporary offline viewing. A download option is not available for every title.

FEATURE 04

Subtitles and audio

Supported content may offer subtitles, alternate audio, or audio descriptions. The available options depend on the title and device.

FEATURE 05

Rent and buy options

Some titles may be offered for separate rental or purchase. Review the price and viewing terms on the title page before confirming a transaction.

FEATURE 06

Multiple devices

Prime Video supports a variety of compatible devices. Playback quality and other features can differ depending on the hardware and connection.

03 / ANDROID SETUP

How to Install Prime Video on Android

Installing the official app is straightforward. Using the Google Play Store also makes it easier to identify the publisher and receive supported app updates.

  1. 01
    Open Google Play Store

    Open the Play Store on your Android phone or tablet and make sure your internet connection is working.

  2. 02
    Find the official app

    Search for Prime Video and check the app listing and publisher details before continuing.

  3. 03
    Check compatibility

    Review the listing to confirm that the app is available for your device and Android version.

  4. 04
    Install and open the app

    Tap Install, wait for the installation to finish, and open Prime Video.

  5. 05
    Sign in and review membership details

    Follow the app's account instructions. Before subscribing, renting, or buying anything, check the price and terms displayed for your region.

If the app does not appear in your store, check device compatibility and official support information. Avoid installing unfamiliar files from third-party download sites.

04 / OFFLINE VIEWING

How Does Prime Video Offline Viewing Work?

Offline downloads can be useful when you expect to be without a reliable internet connection. On supported devices, Prime Video lets you download eligible films and television episodes through the official app.

To check whether a title is available, open its details page in the app and look for the download option. For television series, download options may appear for a season or individual episodes.

Keep in mind that not every title can be downloaded. Viewing periods and other restrictions can also apply, so downloaded content should be treated as temporary offline access rather than a permanent copy.

Before travelling: Download eligible titles over a reliable connection and check the app's download information. Do not assume that every film or episode will remain available offline indefinitely.

05 / MEMBERSHIP AND COST

Is Prime Video Free?

Prime Video is not universally free. Depending on the market and offer, access may require an eligible subscription, while some titles can have separate rental, purchase, or add-on charges.

A subscription also does not guarantee that every title or third-party channel is included. Check the information shown on the official service for your country before signing up or making a payment.

06 / DEVICE COMPATIBILITY

Which Devices Support Prime Video?

Prime Video is available on a range of devices, including supported Android and iOS mobile devices, computers, selected smart TVs, and compatible streaming equipment.

However, support is not identical across every model. Video quality, audio options, profiles, and download functionality can depend on the device and the app version.

If a feature is missing, first check whether your device is supported and whether the app needs an update. The official support pages provide the most reliable information about current device requirements.

07 / SAFETY CHECKLIST

Things to Check Before Using the App

A few simple checks can help you avoid misleading listings, unexpected charges, and unnecessary security risks.

  • Use an official app store or the service's official website.
  • Check the publisher before installing an application.
  • Review subscription, rental, and purchase prices before confirming payment.
  • Keep your app and device software updated.
  • Use a strong, unique password for your account.
  • Never share verification codes or account details with strangers.
  • Avoid unofficial APK files and websites promising unauthorised free access.

08 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Prime Video FAQs

Is Prime Video the same as Amazon Prime?

Prime Video is Amazon's video streaming service. How it relates to an Amazon Prime membership depends on the country and the available subscription options. Check the official terms for your region.

Can I watch Prime Video offline?

Selected titles can be downloaded through supported apps and devices. Download availability and viewing restrictions vary by title and subscription.

Can I download every movie?

No. The download option is available only for eligible titles. Check the title details in the official app.

Why is a movie asking for an additional payment?

Some titles may be offered separately for rental or purchase, or through an additional channel subscription. Review the price and terms before confirming.

Why is Prime Video unavailable on my device?

Availability can depend on your device, software version, and region. Check the official compatibility information and update the app if an update is available.

Is MovieNova an official Amazon website?

No. MovieNova is an independent information website and is not affiliated with Amazon or Prime Video.
