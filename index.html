NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about MovieNova and the information available on this website.

Is MovieNova an official app publisher? +

No. MovieNova is an independent information website. It is not affiliated with third-party app publishers unless a formal relationship is explicitly established.

Where should I get an app? +

Prefer an official app store or the developer's verified website. Check the publisher and avoid unfamiliar download sources.

Does MovieNova provide installation guides? +

MovieNova provides app information and setup guidance. Available guides depend on the apps covered on the website.

Is MovieNova free to access? +

You can browse the information published on this website. Individual third-party apps may have their own prices, subscriptions, or usage conditions.
